QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to common questions about booking and playing at Chetpet Arena.

How far in advance can I book a slot?
▼
You can book slots up to 14 days in advance. Our online calendar shows real-time availability, and you can secure your preferred date and timing instantly with full payment.
What payment methods do you accept?
▼
We accept all major payment methods including UPI, NetBanking, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards. All transactions are secure and encrypted.
Do you refund if there's rain or bad weather?
▼
Yes, we offer full refunds or rescheduling if the match is affected by rain or weather conditions. Our surface has excellent drainage for light to moderate rainfall.
What are the ground rules and equipment requirements?
▼
We follow standard cricket rules. Spike shoes are mandatory. We provide all essential equipment. Teams must bring their own balls and batting gear.
Can we book for tournaments or corporate events?
▼
Absolutely! We offer special packages for tournaments and corporate events. Contact us for custom pricing and availability for multi-day bookings.
Are spectators and supporters allowed?
▼
Yes! We have dedicated spectator stands. Family and friends are welcome to watch. Seating is on a first-come, first-served basis.
